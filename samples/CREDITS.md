# Sample footage

The `interview-*` renders are captioned versions of Creative Commons footage from Wikimedia Commons, re-framed to 9:16 and trimmed:

| Render | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `interview-two-people.eclipse.mp4` (40 s excerpt from 1:00) | [Interview with Jeff Nippard](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interview_with_Jeff_Nippard_%E2%80%93_Science_communication_and_neck_training_(science-based_bodybuilding).webm) | JPS Health & Fitness | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |
| `interview-cutaways.eclipse.mp4` | [Wikipedia 20 – Darya & Avner](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikipedia_20_-_Darya_%26_Avner.webm) | Wikimedia Foundation | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), and so is this render |

Changes: cropped to 9:16 around the speaker, trimmed, captions added.

`input/synthetic-portrait.mp4` is generated (macOS `say` speech over a gradient) and ships with its transcript, for running the pipeline without a key.
